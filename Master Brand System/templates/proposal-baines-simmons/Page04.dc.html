Management system assistance

[Client] wish to lodge an agreed sum with Baines Simmons, to be drawn down as and when management system advice is needed. This gives the core leadership team access to [Consultant] and the wider Baines Simmons team for coaching and advice between the in-person tranches. Each request for support will be charged at a minimum of one hour, with any further time charged in 30-minute increments.

Baines Simmons will endeavour to respond to each request as soon as practically possible, and in any case within two working days. Working days are Monday to Friday, UK time, excluding UK public holidays.

Baines Simmons will provide a statement of time used and the remaining balance monthly or on request, and will let you know when the balance falls below 5 hours so it can be topped up if required.

Funds will be valid for 12 months from the date of payment, after which any unused balance will expire. At any point within those 12 months, [Client] may convert some or all of the remaining balance into credit towards Baines Simmons in-company or Academy training courses.

Deliverables

Participants will receive reference materials to support the sessions, along with access to tools including our SMART map, which sets out the functional areas of a management system and how to measure their effectiveness, and our Just Culture decision aid. The key deliverable is an agreed action plan answering one question: what are we going to do differently? This will be reviewed and updated during the follow-on tranches.

Beyond the programme

Lasting change depends on what happens after the programme ends. We recommend a continuous assurance programme, in which we return periodically to review progress against the action plan and the SMART map, helping [Client] focus on the improvements that will make the biggest difference in each period. Over time, the aim is for [Client]’s own teams to take on this assurance through their internal evaluation programme, with Baines Simmons providing independent oversight.

We also offer a Management System Performance Assessment (MSPA), which provides a structured baseline of where the organisation is today. If you would like more information about an MSPA, please get in touch.
