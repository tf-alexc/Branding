## Logos and Usage

### Primary Logo
- Logomark (icon): blue (brand Midnight) or white
- Logotype: Titillium Web font
- The primary logo = logomark + logotype together
- Always spell "TrustFlight" with capital T and capital F

**DO:** Use on white backgrounds (dark Midnight version) or on Midnight backgrounds (white version)
**DON'T:** Use the logo against bright backgrounds unless it's a brand product palette. Never change the logo colour unless it's Midnight or white. Do not recreate.

### Secondary (Stacked) Logo
- Use only when horizontal space is not available
- Avoid at small sizes
- Always use files from the logo pack — do not recreate

### Clear Space
- Use the uppercase T height as the clear space unit around the logo on all sides
- Do not place the logo on a busy background

## Communication Channels & Etiquette

### Social Media (LinkedIn)
- Represent TrustFlight with professionalism and respect
- Engage authentically; contribute to thought leadership relevant to aviation
- Align content with TrustFlight's core values: safety, trust, and respect
- DO NOT post political, religious, or divisive content on accounts affiliated with TrustFlight
- DO NOT share negative or inflammatory comments about individuals, competitors, or organisations

### Email (Microsoft Outlook)
- Always email from your @trustflight.com / @bainessimmons.com / @trustredline.co.uk / @kenyoninternational.com address
- Default font: Arial, no bigger than 12pt
- Clean formatting — no personal logos, quotes, or off-brand fonts
- Use To: for action recipients; CC: only for those who genuinely need visibility
- Respond within 24 hours (internal) or 1-2 business days (external)
- Email signatures are automatically applied via Admin 365 / Azure (pulled from HiBob)
- Contact: techops@trustflight.com for email issues

### Slack
- Primary tool for internal communication
- Use public channels for visibility; threads to keep conversations organised
- Do not use Slack for confidential or sensitive topics
- Ask permission before starting a huddle

### Microsoft Teams
- Used for all internal and external meetings
- Meetings should have clear agendas, punctual starts, professional conduct
- Use branded Teams backgrounds where possible (sets available per business)
- Contact: techops@trustflight.com for Teams issues

## Media Policy

### Media Requests
- All media inquiries directed to: marketing@trustflight.com
- Employees must NOT respond to journalists or provide statements unless explicitly authorised

### Authorised Spokespeople
- Only designated individuals (executive team or appointed subject matter experts) may speak with media
- If contacted by a journalist, refer them to Marketing without offering comment

### Press Interviews
Approved spokespeople must:
- Coordinate all interviews through Marketing & Communications
- Be briefed in advance on messaging, tone, and topics
- Avoid speculation, off-the-record commentary, or discussing confidential/unreleased information
- All quotes must be reviewed and approved by Marketing & Communications before publication

## Products and Services Reference

### Technology Pillar (TrustFlight direct)

**Tech Log** — Electronic Technical Logbook for aviation maintenance and flight operations. 20,000+ active users. Cloud-based, mobile-first.

**Centrik 5** — Safety, Quality, and Risk Management platform. 180,000+ aviation professionals globally. Integrates Safety, Quality, Risk, Training, Documents, Meetings, Workflows, Asset Management.

**Part-IS Solution** — EASA Part-IS Compliance service. Gap assessments, documentation, technical controls, training, audit preparation.

**CAMO Services** — Continuing Airworthiness Management. Approved by UK CAA and EASA.

**AOC Services** — Air Operator Certificate lifecycle management, from initial setup to ongoing compliance.

**Document Publishing** — Regulatory documentation using advanced XML publishing. Supports EASA, FAA, ICAO, Transport Canada.

**Postholder Service** — EASA Accountable Manager and Post Holder Secondments.

**Consulting** — Regulatory compliance and operational excellence guidance.

**Centrik Training** — QMS/SMS training programmes, virtual/in-person/blended delivery, TrustFlight Certification.
